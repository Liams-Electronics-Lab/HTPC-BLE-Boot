HTPC-BLEBoot ESP32-S3 Firmware v1.01
=====================================

First-Time Setup
----------------
1. After flashing, the ESP32 will create a WiFi AP:
   SSID: HTPC-BLEBoot
   Password: 

2. Connect to the AP and navigate to:
   http://192.168.4.1

3. Configure your WiFi credentials and BLE device settings
